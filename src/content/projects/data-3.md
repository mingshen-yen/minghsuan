Near-fault velocity pulses from the 2023 Turkey earthquake doublet (Mw 7.8 + Mw 7.5) showed significant variability in properties and orientations that a 3D dynamic rupture simulation could not fully reproduce. The study concludes that directivity effects alone are insufficient to explain pulse characteristics — local slip heterogeneities, fault geometry, and site effects (e.g., basin effects) are equally critical factors.
